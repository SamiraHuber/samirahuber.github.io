When I attended my first startup pitch event, I was often the only woman in the room. Over time, I met more and more inspiring female founders, engineers, and researchers, and I realized how much representation matters.

If you are a woman or non-binary person considering a career in tech or entrepreneurship, here is my advice:

- **Find your community.** Networks like Female StartAperitivo and Netlight's Code Pub showed me that I was not alone.
- **Apply anyway.** You do not need to meet 100% of the requirements to try.
- **Share your story.** Your perspective is valuable, and speaking up helps others do the same.
- **Support others.** Success is more meaningful when we lift each other up.

The tech industry still has a long way to go, but every diverse team makes it a little better.
