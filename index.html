Hi, I am Samira Huber.

PhD Student in Robotics.

I am a PhD candidate in embodied AI and robot learning at the Visual Computing and Artificial Intelligence Lab at Kiel University, where I research world models and foundation models for real-world robotic autonomy. I develop and evaluate learning-based systems in simulation and deploy them on physical robots using ROS2. My first-author work has been published at CoRL and IROS.

Before my PhD, I co-founded EcoCheck, where I build AI-powered CO₂ accounting systems, and worked as a freelance software and AI developer. That background shapes how I approach research: translating complex technology into systems that work in practice.

PUBLICATIONS

CoRL 2026

COOL: Curiosity-Driven Object Ownership Learning for Personalized Robotic Assistance

Samira Huber1,*, Ruben Hammele1,*, Sören Pirk1

1Kiel University, Germany  ·  *Equal contribution

We present COOL, a robotic framework for autonomously learning object ownership from everyday observations and maintaining a long-term spatial memory of its environment. COOL uses an agent-based curiosity-driven data collection strategy that guides the robot toward the most promising locations to gain information and refresh stale observations, enabling ownership-conditioned navigation and task execution.

IROS 2026

HUMEMBR: Learning Human Routines for Predictive Embodied Navigation

Samira Huber1, Klaas Pelzer1, Duc M. Nguyen2, Xuesu Xiao2, Sören Pirk1

1Kiel University, Germany  ·  2George Mason University, USA

We introduce Human-Centered Memory for Embodied Robots (HUMEMBR), a system for embodied question answering and routine-conditioned navigation. HUMEMBR integrates continuous memory construction with parallel retrieval and querying, enabling embodied agents to accumulate structured representations of human routines while supporting interactive, user-driven queries.
