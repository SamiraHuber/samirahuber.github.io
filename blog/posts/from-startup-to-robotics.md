Building EcoCheck taught me how to turn an idea into a product used by thousands of people. After several years of entrepreneurship, I decided to go back to research and start a PhD in robotics.

The transition was not obvious. Startups reward speed, MVPs, and short feedback loops. Research rewards depth, rigor, and long-term thinking. Yet both share the same core skill: asking the right questions and iterating until the answer works.

In my new role at the Visual Computing and Artificial Intelligence Lab, I work on vision-language-action models and embodied AI. I am excited to bring my product perspective into robotics research and to build systems that can actually help people in the real world.
