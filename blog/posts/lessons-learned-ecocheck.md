EcoCheck started as a small idea during my Master's: make the climate impact of groceries visible so consumers can make better choices. Three years later, it became an app used by over ten thousand people, featured in newspapers, TV, and radio.

Here are the most important lessons I learned along the way:

- **Start simple.** Our first prototype was a spreadsheet. It was enough to validate that users cared.
- **Talk to users early.** Every major feature in EcoCheck came from a conversation with a shopper, a retailer, or a journalist.
- **Build a strong team.** Co-founders, mentors, and advisors matter more than any single idea.
- **Persistence beats perfection.** Most overnight successes are actually years of showing up.

The journey is far from over, but I am proud of what we built and grateful for everyone who supported us.
